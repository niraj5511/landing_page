Our Story

Brew & Bean started as a tiny corner stall with one grinder and a big idea: great coffee should feel personal. We source beans directly from small farms and roast them in-house every morning.

Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso or settling in for the afternoon, there's always a seat and a warm cup waiting for you.
